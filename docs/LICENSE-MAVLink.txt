MAVLink message definitions: licence notice

The XML files in tools/mavlink-xml/ are the checksum-pinned common and
ardupilotmega dialects and their complete include graph from
https://github.com/mavlink/mavlink at commit
12c898d88ce18d24e8caf6338596e98c5005bc97 (message_definitions/v1.0).
`SHA256SUMS` and `tools/fetch_mavlink_xml.py` record the source hashes.
`docs/js/msgdefs.js` and `src/mavframe_crc_extra_common.h` are generated from
these definitions and cross-checked with pymavlink 2.4.50.
